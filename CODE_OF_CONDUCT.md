# Community Conduct

We want project discussion and review to stay useful, technical, and welcoming to contributors of different experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Give specific, reproducible feedback.
- Assume good faith while still requiring evidence for technical claims.
- Respect authorship, licenses, privacy, and security boundaries.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets or private information, spam, and knowingly submitting material you do not have permission to redistribute are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and participation that violate these rules. Serious security or privacy issues should be reported privately as described in `SECURITY.md` rather than litigated in public threads.

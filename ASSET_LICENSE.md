- original project-specific modifications layered on top of permissively licensed source assets.

You may inspect these assets, run the project, and contribute changes through this repository. Public visibility alone does not grant a separate right to redistribute, resell, extract, or reuse the original project assets in another product.

## MakeHuman / MPFB-derived content

The character pipeline uses MakeHuman/MPFB core assets and exports. MakeHuman Community documents its core graphical assets and exports as CC0. Those upstream CC0 portions remain available under their upstream terms. Project-authored additions and modifications are covered by the project asset terms above to the extent they are independently protectable.

Third-party MakeHuman community assets, if ever added, must be recorded individually in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`; do not assume every community asset is CC0.

## Generated audio

The committed BGM was generated with Google Gemini/Lyria according to the project owner. Google states that it does not claim ownership over original generated content, while users remain responsible for complying with applicable law and third-party rights. The project does not grant the BGM a separate open-source asset license through `LICENSE`.

Because copyright treatment of AI-generated material can vary by jurisdiction and by the human contribution involved, this file does not claim exclusive copyright in portions that applicable law may not protect.

If you want to reuse a specific non-code asset outside this project and its license is unclear, open a discussion or contact the repository owner before redistribution.
